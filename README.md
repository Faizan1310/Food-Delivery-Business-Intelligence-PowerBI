# 🍽️ Food Delivery Business Intelligence Dashboard

> An end-to-end Business Intelligence project built with Power BI to analyze food delivery operations, customer behavior, restaurant performance, and business trends through interactive data visualization.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Food Delivery Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard transforms raw food delivery data into meaningful business insights across multiple dimensions, including:

- Revenue and order performance
- Customer behavior and purchasing patterns
- Restaurant performance
- Delivery and operational efficiency
- Cuisine and category analysis
- Ratings and customer experience
- Advanced business insights

The project is designed to demonstrate practical skills in **data analysis, data modeling, DAX, Power Query, and business intelligence dashboard development**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor overall business performance using key KPIs
- Analyze revenue and order trends
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Identify high-performing restaurants and cuisines
- Analyze restaurant ratings and their relationship with revenue
- Evaluate delivery and operational performance
- Identify cancellation and delivery issues
- Provide actionable insights for business decision-making

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. 🏠 Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of the overall business performance.

**Key metrics include:**

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Average Rating
- Average Delivery Time
- Order performance
- Revenue trends
- Restaurant/cuisine performance

This page acts as the main executive dashboard for quickly understanding business performance.

---

### 2. 👥 Customer Analytics

Analyzes customer behavior and purchasing patterns.

Key areas include:

- Customer distribution
- Customer ordering behavior
- Customer revenue contribution
- Order frequency
- Customer segmentation
- Customer-related performance trends

---

### 3. 🍽️ Restaurant Performance

Evaluates restaurant-level performance and identifies important business patterns.

Analysis includes:

- Restaurant revenue
- Order volume
- Restaurant ratings
- Cuisine performance
- Revenue vs. rating
- Top-performing restaurants
- Restaurant-level trends

---

### 4. 🚚 Delivery & Operations

Focuses on operational efficiency and delivery performance.

Key areas include:

- Average delivery time
- Delivery trends
- Order status
- Cancellation analysis
- Operational performance
- Delivery performance across different locations/restaurants

---

### 5. 🤖 Advanced Insights

Provides deeper analytical insights beyond basic KPI reporting.

This page focuses on identifying relationships and patterns within the data, helping convert descriptive analytics into meaningful business insights.

---

### 6. 🔎 Restaurant Details

Provides a detailed restaurant-level view for deeper analysis.

Users can investigate individual restaurant performance across multiple metrics and dimensions.
